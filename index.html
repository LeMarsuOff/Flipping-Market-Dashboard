    Optimal RR Analysis
    ?
    Simulation mode

    BE Fallback (default): when a winning trade reached TP1 (2.4R) but not the simulated TP, it is removed from the dataset (treated as a break-even exit) and counted only in the BE Trades KPI. If the trade didn't even reach TP1, it's counted as a loss in the simulation.

    Strict Loss: any winning trade that didn't reach the simulated TP is counted as a full loss (−1R), remaining in the dataset. More realistic when simulating a fixed SL at −1R with no BE management.

    BE Fallback reduces the effective sample size above TP1 and is optimistic. Strict Loss preserves the sample and is conservative.

    Simulator assumptions
    • Fixed SL at −1R (R-multiples convention)
    • BE armed after TP1 hit (universal, non-disableable)
    • If rrMax < TP1 → −1R full size
    • Unreached legs → exit at 0R (BE)
    • TP1 must be ≥ 1R (path-order vs rrMax)
    • Full-TP baseline = 100% at TP final
    • Bootstrap CI95%: 500 iterations (100 in sweep mode)
    ⚠ Standardized TP1/TP2 across all trades. Brute-force comparator — not a forward optimization.
